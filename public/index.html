How bidding works

Bid, or set a max

Place a bid, or give a maximum and the auto-bidder raises for you only as much as needed to stay in front.

No last-second sniping

Any bid in the final 30 seconds adds 30 seconds, so everyone gets a fair chance to respond.

The server decides

Bids are processed one at a time per lot, timed by the server clock, written to a log, and checkable on the System page.
